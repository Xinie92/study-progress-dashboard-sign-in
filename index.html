01
Getting started

Building understanding

These are reflection prompts, not a score. Update the gate on any course whenever your understanding changes.

USE YOUR DASHBOARD ON EACH DEVICE

Set up study sync

Your dashboard needs a Supabase project to hold your account and study data online. No project is connected yet.

  1. Create a Supabase project in your account.
  2. Run the SQL in supabase/schema.sql in its SQL Editor.
  3. Copy its Project URL and publishable key into cloud-config.js.

After that, this window will let you create an account or sign in on both devices.

This account’s study data syncs through your Supabase database.
